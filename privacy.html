ReelPilot privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ReelPilot is a personal desktop app used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, ReelPilot uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel, list the channel's videos and playlists, and change their titles, descriptions and privacy.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and everything the app makes stay on the owner's computer. ReelPilot runs no servers, does not sell or share data, and does not send Google user data to anyone else.

Google API Services

ReelPilot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ReelPilot uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the app's local data deleted by deleting its data folder.
